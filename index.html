About Me

I work in industrial automation and control, with hands-on experience in PLC programming, motion control, machine vision, industrial communication, and machine integration.

I look at each machine as a complete system: mechanics, electrical hardware, controls, and software. I learn new technologies independently and develop practical solutions from the initial idea through to a working system.

I develop software in Python, C#, Java, and VB.NET. I also use modern AI tools to support software development and help solve technical problems.

My Philosophy

I believe that sharing knowledge, discoveries, and practical solutions strengthens our own understanding and helps others move forward.

There is no need to reinvent the wheel when we can learn from one another and build something better.
